# Ideas

the main protagonist is the original one, but he doesnt know it and neither does anyone else. 

before the universes existed, before time existed, not even the protagonist existed, but he wished nothing more than to simply exist. in his wish to exist he creates himself and the universe, however in order for the universe not to collapse under the logical contradiction that something non-existing created itself out of nothing, he must not know he is the original one that created everything. 

so the protagonist lives in a town and on his 15th birthday he is allowed to adventure into the crypt near his town, where big treasures are said to be found. only the strongest ones are allowed to reach into the crypt. a crypt is an underground dungeon with multiple floors and only one crypt is known, but there are multiple. each crypt reveals parts of a puzzle to regain the protagonists consciousness. in the last crypt there will be a sarcophagus of the original one (the protagonist doesn't know he is the original one) and as he opens it and stares into his own eyes, the whole universe collapses and turns into nothingness, destroying the protagonists wish to exist and turns into pure despair.

## The Original One

"Before the universe and before time, before light and matter, there was nothing - an unimaginably vast, infinite emptyness, devoid of anything, so unfulfilled it created only one thing: The unsatiable desire, the unfulfillable wish - burning, tearing, supressing everything else - to be, to exist. I don't remember when I first started to feel, yet when I started to see and hear, within fractions of immediacy my yearning has created me and everything surrounding, sending me into non-existence once again, me, the original one."


## Town and Crypt Entrance

The town knows none of this. It knows one thing, and that is all the player - you - is told at the start: the crypt beyond was built before humans roamed the land and no one knows who built it or how it was built. great treasure and power lies inside.

The gate has stood open for as long as anyone remembers, and it has taken many. On your fifteenth birthday you earn the right to go down. Some turn back from the upper floors with a relic or a scar. Only the strongest get deep. Many of them never come up again, and those who do come back quiet and for days don't answer to their own names. Nobody ever has reached the bottom.

Strange is what happens to the dead. A name is carved into the gate before each descent, never after, because the town learned long ago that after it is too late. When someone dies below, the people above begin to lose them: first the face, then the voice, their scent, the way they moved. Only an idea persisted, small enough to feed an ever-growing hunger to remember, to fill a gap that reaches deep into the heart. Hundreds of names are cut into that gate. Nobody can say whose they were, but everyone longs to linder the unnamable ache.

## The Crypt's levels

The crypt has multiple floors, and after a certain amout of floors, there is some kind of boss fight that lets you access the next "level". They differ thematically and each level unlocks a teleport in town. Each level also provides new abilities that unlock stuff in previous crypts. 



### 1. First Level: Recent History
Well maintained stonework, yet unreadable memorial plaques, and dim lantern light.

Traps, vermin, old bones. Lower floors of this level have some cracked stone and faceless zombies and skeletons attack. They are the dead of the gate.

The first level holds one piece of the buried knowledge: a collection of orbs, each containing a memory fragment and engraved with a name and another one, glowing golden with no name on it.

The names are the ones from the entrance and some can trigger new dialogues with town people. Some immediately after obtaining, some maybe later on, after certain other events have happened.

Every name on the gate becomes a person again, with a face and a voice, and the faceless get their faces back as he looks at them. He is now the only one alive who can mourn them, and he walks out past all of them. Yet, time has turned them feral and they want to end his life. Now with a face.

After that he is welcomed as a hero. Then he speaks the names of the dead and nobody knows them, not even their own families. People repeat a sentence they said a minute ago. An alley he has walked all his life is a blank wall, and always was.

### 2. Second Level: Ossuaries and Plage Pits
Tighter corridors, walls lined with thousands of stacked bones, crumbling mortar, and stagnant air.
Unstable floors that collapse into deeper shafts, toxic spores, and flooded cisterns from the city’s sewer system above.

When beating the level, the golden orb reveals a name: "Eyo, the original one" and it opens a crypt with a skeleton inside, holding a scroll containing knowledge that the crypt was built by the original one.

In town, people's characters come loose. The gentle neighbour has a sharp tongue, the miser gives his money away, and his quiet father laughs and talks like a stranger. Nobody remembers them being different. Some answer him before he has asked the question.

### 3. Third level: Ancient Necropolis / Pre-Humanity Era
Massive sarcophagi of ancient kings, and faint magical wards.
Liches, wights, ethereal beings and ancient constructs still following orders from a fallen empire.

In town, fewer people, and nobody misses the missing. Houses have fronts and no backs. People stand still when he isn't looking at them. They have begun to look alike, and they all blink the way he does. 

### 4. Fourth level: Grave of the Original One
The stonework of the ancient necropolis suddenly stops, giving way to smooth, seamless obsidian, raw starlight, or architecture that defies geometry.

Enemies could be "nightmares" or raw manifestations of creation (beings of pure light, shadow, or unformed matter).

> The Sarcophagus

At the bottom stands a sarcophagus. Similar to the one on the first level. He opens it and the golden orb shatters.

Inside lies himself, eyes open: the original one.

They look at each other, the whole universe collapses and turns into nothingness, destroying the protagonists wish to exist and turns into pure despair. Time ended with the world, so there is no later in which this could ease. What remains is despair, complete and without end.
